SPELL CASTERS

A co-op dungeon crawl where every spell is Morse code.

Move W A S D or arrow keys
Tap Morse Numpad 5 (or Space): short press is a dot, long press is a dash
Cast pause to finish the letter, then aim with Numpad 7 8 9 4 6 1 2 3
Block enemy spells by casting the same letter at them
Dash Numpad 0   Cancel Numpad . / Backspace   Cheat sheet H   Settings Esc
Items (Angband keys) i inventory, e equipment, g pick up, q quaff, r read, t take off, I inspect, k destroy. Wear (w) and drop (d) from the inventory list
Surface You start in a village: walk up to the shop's door to buy and sell, or your house's door to store things. Floors stay as you left them; only enemies come back. Stairs go up and down. A Scroll of Recall takes the party to the surface and back down
Boss The Bellwraith guards the stairs. Its name is shown only in Morse. Its bell shield blocks spells: listen to the letter its bell rings and cast that to break it
On a phone or tablet hold it sideways. Left half: put a finger down and drag to walk. Right half: tap for a dot, hold for a dash, swipe to cast that way. Tap your own caster for the item ring. Tap the left side without dragging to pick up items
Duel Two casters on a floating platform, first to 3 rounds. Any letter goes, the same HP for both, no gear. Block a spell by casting the same letters at it: cast T at an incoming TEA and only EA keeps coming
Save Ctrl+S. Lobbies also save every 30 seconds, on each new floor and when everyone leaves
Turn your device sideways to play

Settings

Dot = ms. Hold longer than ms for a dash.

How long you must stop tapping before the letter is finished. Raise it if letters get cut off.

Calm and quiet while you explore; it grows more urgent as your HP drops and hostile spells close in. M mutes it.

Rings show what the server really counts as a hit: a spell hits you when its white dot enters your ring, and two spells meet when their dashed rings touch. B toggles them.
